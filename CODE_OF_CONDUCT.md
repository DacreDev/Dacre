# Code of conduct

Dacre is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep it a place where anyone can ask a question, report a bug or send a change without being made to feel small.

## Expected

- Be patient and assume good intent.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Give feedback you would want to receive: specific, kind and actionable.
- Respect people's time. Search before you open an issue, and keep threads on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the contact on the [DacreDev GitHub profile](https://github.com/DacreDev). Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break this code. Serious or repeated violations lead to a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
